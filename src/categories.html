Timers are filed under the category picked in the menu, and the countdown wears its color. The share is how much of your timed work you want each one to take. Archiving a category keeps its time in Focus time, takes it out of the menu and the goals, and leaves its share unassigned. Restoring it brings back the share it had. The shares have to add up to 100% before you can save, and Redistribute to 100% scales them there in proportion. A category left at 0% also stays out of the menu and the goals.
